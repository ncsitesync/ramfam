Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains, in plain language, what information RamFam Builders collects when you visit our website, request an estimate, or contact us, and how we use and protect it. We're a small, family-owned business, and we treat your information the way we'd want ours treated.

Who we are

This website is operated by RamFam Builders, LLC ("RamFam Builders," "we," "us," or "our"), a family-owned home builder serving Eastern North Carolina. You can reach us at (910) 385-7680 or info@ramfambuilders.com.

This policy is effective as of October 15, 2026.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you fill out our free estimate or contact form, we collect what you enter, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Phone number and email address
  • The service you're interested in (custom home, remodel, concrete, septic)
  • Project details you choose to share, such as location, timeline, or budget

If you call or text us, we'll have your phone number and anything you tell us during the conversation.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical information when you visit, such as your browser type, device type, general location based on IP address, the pages you view, and the date and time of your visit. If we add website analytics in the future, they would collect this same kind of information about how the site is used.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your estimate request or question
  • Schedule site visits, consultations, and work
  • Communicate with you about your project by phone, text, or email
  • Keep records needed to run our business, such as estimates and contracts
  • Keep our website working and make it more useful

We won't add you to a marketing list without your permission.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run the website and the estimate form, such as our website host and form or email services. They may use your information only to provide those services to us.
  • With people working on your project, such as subcontractors or suppliers, when they need it to do the work you've hired us for.
  • When required by law, for example to respond to a valid legal request, or to protect our rights, our customers, or the public.

Third-party links and services

Our website links to and works with services run by other companies, including Facebook (our page and embedded videos), Google (reviews and maps), and our website hosting provider. When you use those services or follow those links, their own privacy policies apply, not this one. We encourage you to read them.

Cookies and analytics

Our website itself does not use cookies to track you. Some embedded content, such as a Facebook video, may set its own cookies when it loads, under that company's policy. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

We don't currently use website analytics. If we add them, we'll update this section to say which tools we use and how you can opt out.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, such as secure (HTTPS) connections and limited access to the messages we receive. No method of sending or storing information online is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

We keep your information only as long as we need it to respond to you, complete your project, and meet our legal, tax, and warranty obligations. After that, we delete it or keep it in a form that no longer identifies you.

Your choices

  • Access, correction, or deletion: contact us to ask what information we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We may need to keep some records the law requires us to keep.
  • Texts: reply STOP to any text from us to stop receiving texts.
  • Emails: ask us to stop emailing you, or use the unsubscribe link if an email includes one.

Children's privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be noted here.
